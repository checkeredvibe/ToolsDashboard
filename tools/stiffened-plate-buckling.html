Stiffened Plate Buckling — DNV-CG-0128 CFM

Buckling capacity of a stiffened panel per DNV-CG-0128 (Edition December 2023), Sec.3 closed form method: overall stiffened panel capacity ([3.1]), elementary plate panel capacity ([3.2], Table 3 cases 1/2/15) and stiffener checks for stiffener induced (SI) and plate induced (PI) failure ([3.3.4], Table 5). Outputs the utilization factor η per limit state and the limit buckling stress γc·σ per [2.1.3]. Sign convention of the guideline: compressive stresses are positive. Stresses in N/mm², lateral pressure in kN/m², lengths and thicknesses in mm (net scantlings). All local — no network.

Calculations used & sources

Equations (DNV-CG-0128, Edition December 2023, Sec.3 CFM)

  • Overall stiffened panel capacity ([3.1.1]–[3.1.4]): γ/γGEB = 1; orthotropic stiffnesses D₁₁ = E·Ieff·10⁴/s, D₁₂ = νD₂₂, D₂₂ = E·tp³/(12(1−ν²)), D₃₃ = E·tp³/(12(1+ν)); γGEB,bi minimized over the integer half-wave number n with LB1 = ℓ (0.8ℓ continuous/bracketed), LB2 = 6s; Nx = σx,av(Ap+As)/s with Poisson-corrected σx,av, Ny = c·σytp; pure shear γGEB,τ (two-branch formula, Nxy = τtp); combined γGEB,bi+τ interaction.
  • Plate (EPP) capacity ([3.2.1]): interaction formulae for γc1…γc4 with reduction factors Cx,Cy,Cτ from Table 3 cases 1, 2 and 15 (simply supported), σE = π²E/(12(1−ν²))·(tp/b)², λ = √(ReH,P/(KσE)), coefficients B, e₀ and βp from Table 1; Flong from Table 2 (c = 0.10 flat, 0.30 bulb/T, 0.40 angle when fixed at both ends), Ftran = 1, c₁ per SP-A/SP-B ([3.2.3]); γc = min(γc1…γc4), η = 1/γc.
  • Stiffener capacity ([3.3.4]): (γcσa + σb + σw)S/ReH = 1 solved iteratively for γc (SI: ReH,S, Z at flange top; PI: ReH,P, Z at attached plating); σa = σx(stp+As)/(beff1tp+As); σb = (M₀+M₁+M₂)/(1000Z) with M₀ = FECsl(γ/(γGEB−γ))w₀, w₀ = ℓ/1000, Csl from the global slenderness λG, M₁ = Ci|P|sℓ²/(24·10³) (continuous; /8·10³ sniped), M₂ = Csnipwnaγσx(Ap+As) for sniped ends; torsional stress σw (SI only, σa > 0) with σET minimized over the half-wave number mtor using IP, IT, Iω from Table 5 and the degree of fixation ε.
  • Effective width of attached plating ([3.3.5]): beff1 = Cxb (=σx>0) or b; beff = min(beff1; χss) with χs from ℓeff/s (ℓ/√3 fixed, 0.75ℓ one end fixed, ℓ simply supported).
  • Flat bar reduced web thickness ([3.3.2]): tw,red = tw[1 − (2π²/3)(hw/s)²(1 − beff1/s)] used for As, Z and I.
  • Section idealization: bulb as equivalent angle (DNV-RU-SHIP Pt.3 Ch.3 Sec.7 [1.4.1]); ef = hw (flat), hw−0.5tf (bulb), hw+0.5tf (angle/T).
  • Ultimate capacity and utilization per [2.1.3]/[2.1.4]: the applied combination is scaled proportionally until the governing interaction formula equals 1.0; η = 1/γc, limit buckling stress = γc·σ.
  • γc1 of the plate interaction and γc of the stiffener checks are solved with bisection root-finding; γGEB,bi and σET are minimized over integer half-wave numbers.

Compressive stresses positive, tension negative; net scantlings (100% corrosion addition deducted). The plate check uses the EPP between stiffeners (a = ℓ, b = s, simply supported edges per [3.2.3]); U-type stiffeners, corrugated panels, curved plates and vertically stiffened plating specials (Ftran ≠ 1) are out of scope for v1. Validate against a hand calculation or PULS before professional use.
