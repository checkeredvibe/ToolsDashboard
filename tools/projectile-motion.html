Calculations used & sources

Equations of motion (no air resistance)

  • x(t) = v₀ · cos(θ) · t
  • y(t) = h₀ + v₀ · sin(θ) · t − ½ g t²
  • tflight = (v₀ sinθ + √(v₀² sin²θ + 2 g h₀)) / g (positive root of y(t)=0)
  • R = v₀ cosθ · tflight
  • hmax = h₀ + (v₀ sinθ)² / (2 g)
  • vimpact = √(vx² + vy,impact²), with vy,impact = v₀ sinθ − g tflight

Assumes a point mass, constant gravitational field, flat ground and no aerodynamic drag.
